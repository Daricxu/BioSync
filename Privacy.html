Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Biosync team A/S (“BioSync,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your personal information and handling it responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

Questions? Email us anytime at funbiosync@hotmail.com.

What data we collect

We collect what is needed to provide and improve PulseBurst. This may include:

Information you provide for your profile

  • Nickname or display name
  • Email address
  • Gender (optional)
  • Age (optional)

Health information, with your permission

  • When you play PulseBurst, Apple Watch may send real-time heart-rate readings to the app to calculate the speed of balloon movement.
  • With your permission, PulseBurst may also access heart-rate data in Apple Health as a reference for calculating balloon speed.

Data collected while you use PulseBurst

  • Match ID and user ID
  • Game mode (solo or group)
  • Match start and finish times
  • Minimum and maximum heart rate during each round

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers who help operate or improve PulseBurst. They process information on our behalf and are expected to protect it and follow applicable requirements.

How we keep your data safe

We take security seriously and use industry-standard measures to protect data from unauthorized access, loss, and misuse. These measures include encryption, firewalls, and access controls.

However, no system is completely secure. Please keep your login details private and understand that transmitting data online carries some risk.

Updates to this privacy policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our service and legal requirements. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or through the app. Continuing to use PulseBurst after an update means you agree to the revised policy.

Contact form

If you send us a message through this website, the information you provide—including your name, email, company or team, job function, and message—is sent to funbiosync@hotmail.com using FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Please avoid including sensitive health or other confidential information in a website message.
